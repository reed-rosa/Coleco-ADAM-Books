# Coleco ADAM Books — Annotated Bibliography

A working bibliography of books, guides, and independently published technical literature for the **Coleco ADAM** computer.

**Scope:** This is a bibliographic inventory, **not** a claim that every title was released, nor that every ISBN is verified. The starting point was a 53-entry privately compiled *Adam Books* PDF (circa 2025). Reviewed against web-accessible bibliographies and contemporary ADAM publications on **2026-11-01**. The original entry numbers are intentionally preserved for provenance; suspected duplicates are not silently deleted.

> **Evidence key:** `Source` means reported in the original list; `Cross-listed` means found in another bibliography or contemporary listing; `Direct` means a scanned title/copyright page or contemporary review supports the details. *Cross-listed does not mean publication or ISBN assignment is definitively verified.* This is a **first-pass** research catalog, not a line-by-line physical-copy audit.

## Additional titles for investigation (not in the 53 original entries)

These are **candidates**, not additions that have been conclusively verified as separate physical editions:

- **The Coleco ADAM User’s Encyclopedia** — Book Company; the 1986 resource directory gives ISBN `0-317-05838-X`. In the original document this title was appended to #9 instead of being numbered separately. Confirm the cover, publisher, and publication status.
- **Simon & Schuster Guide to the Coleco ADAM** — Danny Goodman; listed in later archival bibliographies. Confirm physical publication and ISBN.
- **The ADAM Home Computer: An Introduction to SmartBASIC and Applications** — Larry Joel Goldstein, Brady; determine whether a retitle/edition of #14 or a separate work.
- **Z80 Assembly-Language Programming** — Christopher Lampton (Franklin Watts, 1985); appears in an ADAM repository but may be general Z80 literature, so check ADAM-specific coverage before including as an ADAM book.
- **Programming Games for the ColecoVision & ADAM in Assembler** — Tony Cruise; later homebrew title, relevant if expanding scope beyond 1980s publications.
- **The ADAM Resource Directory** — Keith A. Burrous, 1986 second edition; valuable standalone historical reference and source for the list.
- **How to Document Your Software** — Barbara Spear; the 1986 directory says it was written using an ADAM, but it may not be ADAM-specific.

## ISBN checksum warnings

A mechanical ISBN-10 checksum check flagged three numbers repeated in bibliographies: **#11** `0-672-22309-9`, **#21** `0-88134-125-0`, and **#33** `0-88022-866-X`. They are recorded as *reported identifiers*, not verified valid ISBNs. A check digit failure is evidence of a transcription error, not evidence a book did not exist. Consult physical copyright pages before replacing them.

2. **Publication-status audit:** Distinguish *planned/advertised*, *manuscript*, *published*, and *physical copy observed*, especially #13, #16, #21, #24, #38/#48, and #51.

4. **Broaden scope deliberately:** Decide whether ADAM user-group manuals, catalogs, diskette companion books, foreign-language books, and modern homebrew manuals belong in this bibliography.

## Corrections and contributions

If you own a copy, the most helpful evidence is a photo or transcription of the **title page and copyright/ISBN page**. Please open a GitHub Issue with the entry number, proposed change, and source URL or page reference. Do not upload copyrighted book scans without permission.

Changes should record whether an assertion is **verified from a physical scan**, **reported by a historical advertisement**, or **inferred from secondary catalogs**. Do not replace unknown publication dates with guesses or treat a book listing as definitive proof of publication.
